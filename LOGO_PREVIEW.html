Key Specifications

Mark Design

  • Left stem curves away and drops below baseline
  • S-curve crossbar connects both stems
  • Right stem mirrors left with upward curve
  • Saffron node at the end of left curl
  • All strokes: round caps and joins

Usage Rules

  • ✓ Scale both axes together (no stretching)
  • ✓ Minimum 16px on screen, 8mm in print
  • ✗ Don't use big master below 40px
  • ✗ Don't recolor stems
  • ✗ Don't add drop shadows or outlines
