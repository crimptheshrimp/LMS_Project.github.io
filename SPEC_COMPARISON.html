PDF Specification vs Implementation Comparison

What the PDF Specified

The learninghub brand specification document provided these key design requirements:

Mark Design (from PDF):
"An L and an H drawn as one ligature. The left stem drops below the baseline and curls away, carrying the L; a swooping crossbar joins it to the right stem. A saffron node sits just off the end of the curl, which is where the hub idea returns: the stroke flows towards a point rather than stopping."
  • Left stem drops below baseline and curves away
  • Right stem is the vertical H
  • Swooping (S-curve) crossbar connects both stems
  • Saffron node positioned at the end of the left curl
  • Two masters: Big (40px+) and Small (under 40px)
  • Three colors: Ink Pine (#10262B), Saffron (#E9A13B), Bone (#FAF8F5)
  • Saffron appears ONLY on the node, nowhere else

📋 PDF: Big Master (40px+)

Specifications:
